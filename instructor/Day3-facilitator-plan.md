## Where people get stuck

- **Steps 1 and 2:** finding the Instructions box. Initials in the lower left, Settings, Your Claude, Instructions. Show it on screen, slowly, twice. Anyone whose app still has a Cowork tab uses Settings, Cowork, Global instructions.
- **Step 3:** they paste the prompt into a chat without clicking Work in a folder first. Check screens as you roam.
- **Step 6:** the guardrails make Claude show a plan and wait. Say it out loud before they paste: "It will stop and show you a plan. Read it, then say looks good, go ahead."
- **Step 7:** they keep going in the same chat. A new chat with the folder selected is what proves the folder did the work. If a total is off, have them ask Claude to show the math line by line.
- **Step 8:** short answers produce a thin HQ. Coach question 3 (the steps) and question 4 (what stays with you).
- **Step 9:** people reach for a real customer drawing. Point at the four questions first.
- **Step 10:** work accounts blocked by IT, and sign in windows hiding behind the app. Highest failure rate of the day. Pair people up.

## Pairing and pace

Clayton roams during every hands on block. Cap one on one help at two minutes, then pair the person with a neighbor who is done. Anyone who finishes a part early starts the next step in the guide. Fast finishers in Part 4 build a second HQ.

## What to collect

At 11:10, ask three people to read their HQ's Identity paragraph out loud. Write the HQ names on the board. Those are the examples for the next cohort.
